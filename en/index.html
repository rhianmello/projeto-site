How to write a stronger resume

  1. Lead with your name, relevant contact information, location and target role.
  2. Focus on experience relevant to the position, ideally showing measurable achievements rather than duties alone.
  3. List education and skills that support your application; remove information unrelated to the position.
  4. Keep formatting easy to scan, proofread carefully, and check the final PDF before applying.

Tip for US applications: a headshot, birth date and marital status are typically omitted. Requirements vary by country and employer.
